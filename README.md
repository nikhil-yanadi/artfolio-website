# 🎨 Artfolio Website

This repository contains my personal **artfolio website**, a curated collection of my traditional and digital artwork with a strong focus on **high-quality pencil drawings**. It represents my dedication to artistic growth, precision, and attention to detail, showcasing my journey as an artist through carefully crafted sketches and illustrations.

## ✏️ About My Work

I specialize in **pencil-based drawings**, with an emphasis on:

* Realistic detailing
* Smooth and controlled shading techniques
* Depth, contrast, and tonal balance
* Fine textures and clean line work
* Accurate proportions and composition

Each artwork is created with patience and precision, focusing on capturing **real-life depth, emotion, and structure** through graphite. My drawings aim to bring subjects to life by combining **technical accuracy with expressive creativity**.

## 🎯 Artistic Focus

My work primarily explores:

* Realistic sketches and studies
* Portraits (if applicable)
* Object and observational drawings
* Light and shadow exploration
* Texture rendering (skin, fabric, surfaces)

I continuously work on improving my ability to represent **three-dimensional depth on a two-dimensional surface**, which is a core principle of my artistic practice.

## 🧠 Approach & Process

My drawing process involves:

1. Careful observation and reference study
2. Light structural sketching for proportions
3. Gradual layering of shading to build depth
4. Refinement of details and textures
5. Final balancing of contrast and highlights

This structured approach helps me maintain **consistency, clarity, and quality** across all my artworks.
