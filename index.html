Profile

Y Nikhil Arts

I am Y Nikhil, a dedicated portrait artist with over 8 years of experience in drawing, sketching, and professional color pencil artwork.

Art has been a part of my life since childhood, and over the years I’ve developed a strong eye for realism, shading, fine detailing, and lifelike expressions. My goal is to make every portrait feel emotional, personal, and visually striking.

I specialize in hand-drawn portraits, color pencil artworks, and custom commissions that are crafted with precision and patience. Every piece I create is designed to capture the soul, story, and personality of the subject.

With consistent practice and passion, I have built a style that blends realism with smooth color transitions, natural textures, and clean finishing.

My Expertise:
• Realistic Portraits
• Color Pencil Drawings
• Charcoal & Graphite Sketches
• Custom Art Commissions
• Detailed Shading & Skin Tone Work

My Vision:
To create meaningful portraits that people connect with emotionally, and to continue growing as an artist delivering high-quality, memorable artwork.
